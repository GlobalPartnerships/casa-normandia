Deep Rest & Strict Quiet Hours

10:00 PM to 7:00 AM · Rest Priority

Quiet Hours Policy

Casa Normandía is designed for professionals, remote workers, and mindful travelers who value tranquility and productivity. Between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM, absolute quiet is required in all hallways, common staircases, and studios.

  • Keep Smart TVs, music, and voice calls at a moderate level at all times.
  • Avoid slamming doors or making impact noises during night and early morning hours.
  • Parties, social gatherings, or loud noise events are strictly prohibited inside the studios.
🚭 Strict 100% Smoke-Free Policy (Cleaning Charge: $200,000 COP)
Smoking traditional cigarettes, vaping (e-cigarettes), using tobacco, or lighting candles and incense is completely prohibited indoors and in common areas. Any violation incurs an automatic sanitization charge of $200,000 COP to cover professional deep cleaning and ozone air treatment required to remove odors and protect guests with allergies.

Check-in and Check-out Times

  • Check-in: Starting at 3:00 PM. If you expect a late-night arrival after 8:00 PM, please notify us ahead of time so we can ensure seamless entry.
  • Check-out: By 11:00 AM sharp. Timely departure is essential to allow our housekeeping team to thoroughly clean, sanitize, and prepare the studio for the incoming guest.

Responsible Pet-Friendly Protocol

Peaceful Living for Four-Legged Companions

At Casa Normandía, we welcome well-behaved dogs and cats under a responsible ownership agreement to maintain community tranquility and impeccable hygiene:

Deep Sanitation Fee: A single fee of $50,000 COP applies per stay for specialized hypoallergenic post-departure sanitation. Maximum 1 pet per studio with prior notice.

Golden Rules for Pets

  • Always on leash: Dogs must always be kept on a leash in hallways, stairwells, and all shared house areas.
  • Protecting furniture: Out of hygienic respect for future guests with sensitivities, pets are not allowed on beds, bedsheets, or sofas. Please bring their own pet bed or blanket.
  • Outdoor walks: Natural bodily needs must always be attended to outside the property. Please pick up and dispose of waste immediately.
  • Constant supervision: Do not leave pets unattended in the studio if they suffer from separation anxiety, whimper, or bark.
⚠️ Prohibition of Exotic Animals: Under Colombian environmental law and house safety standards, wild birds, reptiles, arachnids, and non-conventional species are strictly not permitted.

Building Security, Keys & Visitors

Peace of Mind & Personal Safety

Main Entrance Door

For everyone's security, the main exterior door must remain closed at all times. Whenever you enter or leave the house, please push the door firmly and verify that the electronic deadbolt and lock have engaged fully.

Visitor Policy

  • Overnight stays are strictly limited to guests registered in the original booking.
  • Punctual daytime business meetings are permitted upon prior request, maintaining a quiet and professional atmosphere.
  • Unregistered visitors are strictly not allowed on the property during night hours, and parties of any kind are forbidden.

Key Drop-off at Check-out

Before leaving, please inspect the studio to ensure no chargers or personal belongings are left behind. Lock your studio door and drop all physical keys into the secure wall mailbox located next to the main exit door.

Legal Compliance & Mandatory Registration

Colombian Tourism Regulations · Immigration Laws

Tourist Accommodation Card (TRA) & Migración Colombia

Under Colombian national law (Law 300 of 1996, Law 2068 of 2020, and Decree 2590 of 2009), all lodging operators are legally obligated to record the identity of every national and international guest.

  • International Travelers: Must be reported to the official SIRE system of Migración Colombia within 24 hours of arrival.
  • Colombian Nationals: Must present an official citizenship ID (Cédula) for digital TRA logging.
  • Minors: Must be accompanied by their legal parents or carry an official authenticated parental travel consent letter in compliance with the Code of Childhood and Adolescence (Law 1098 of 2006). We strictly reject any form of commercial sexual exploitation of children and adolescents (Law 679 of 2001).

Data Privacy Policy (Law 1581 of 2012)

Your personal information, passport copies, and identity documents are treated with strict confidentiality solely for legal tourism compliance, billing, and communication regarding your stay. You may request data updates or rectification at any time by writing to contacto.gpmfsas@gmail.com.
